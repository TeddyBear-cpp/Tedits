questions.

The practical bits, so you can get back to the party.

All policies below are illustrative and need confirmation before a real launch.

How would I book? +

Choose a package and share your event details. A real booking would only be confirmed after availability, pricing and terms are agreed. This concept form does not send enquiries or reserve dates.

Where do you deliver? +

Our sample service area is Bristol and nearby towns within 15 miles. Delivery charges and venue access would be quoted separately. This area is a fictional example, not an active service.

What ages is soft play suitable for? +

The sample packages are designed for ages 0–5, with the recommended range shown on each package. A real provider would confirm equipment suitability, capacity and age separation for your guests.

How much space do I need? +

Allow at least 3 × 3 m for Little Explorer, 4 × 4 m for Happy Hopper or 5 × 5 m for Ultimate Play Day, plus clear access and circulation space. These illustrative footprints must be checked against real equipment.

Can we play outside? +

Our sample policy allows dry, level, sheltered outdoor spaces in suitable weather. An indoor backup would be required. A real operator must set safe wind, rain and temperature limits for their equipment.

Do you stay to supervise? +

This is a delivery-and-collection concept, not a childcare service. The organiser must arrange responsible adult supervision throughout the hire. Supervision ratios and rules need agreement with a real operator.

How is the equipment cleaned? +

The proposed approach is to clean and sanitise equipment and balls between hires, following product instructions, and inspect every item before setup. A real business must establish and publish its actual cleaning process.

What if our plans change? +

An illustrative policy might offer a date change, subject to availability, with at least 14 days’ notice. No deposit, refund or cancellation terms are active here; a real provider must supply written terms before payment.
