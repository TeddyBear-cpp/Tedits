## Design read

- Narrative: clear introduction; substantial real website previews; concise case-study context; services; about; contact.

- Five dials: variance 6, motion 3, density 4, asset dependence 8, brand fidelity 8. Use an orderly spine, one asymmetric hero, large actual project screenshots, brief motion, and the supplied wordmark.

## Proposed design system
- Paper #F3F1E9, charcoal #101210, muted text #626C62, sage #86AD91, dark green #365C43, divider #D2D6CA. The paper surface gives web design its own identity alongside the darker video site.
- Typography: Syne headings; local DM Sans body. Fluid headline sizes and readable body line lengths.
- Spacing: 8px base; 1280px content width; 96px desktop / 64px mobile sections.
- Radius: 3–4px. Minimal borders, no decorative shadow.
- Motion: one-time 14px / 640ms entrances and 180–250ms hover feedback. Reduced motion supported; no autoplay or scroll hijacking.
- Logo: supplied Tedits SVG with separate Studio descriptor, recoloured charcoal/sage for paper. Original geometries and source copies preserved.
