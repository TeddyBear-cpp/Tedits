01 / CATERING & EVENTS

Olive & Ember

INDEPENDENT CONCEPT

A warm, editorial catering website that brings occasions, sample menus, and an enquiry journey together.

Web design · Responsive development

Explore the concept ↗
Behind the design +

The idea

Good food can make a big celebration feel special and an ordinary week feel easier. I imagined a catering business that cared about both, with a website that felt as welcoming as sitting down at its table.

How it took shape

Warm greens, generous food photography, and an unhurried layout set that feeling. Sample menus give visitors something to picture, while the enquiry flow starts with the occasion they have in mind.

Demo project for a fictional business. No client commission, testimonials, or business results are claimed. Stock photos illustrate the concept.

02 / STEEL & FABRICATION

Rivet & Beam

INDEPENDENT CONCEPT

An industrial website concept with a clear material catalogue and a flexible enquiry flow for standard supplies or custom fabrication.

Web design · Responsive development

Explore the concept ↗
Behind the design +

The idea

Some people know the exact steel section they need. Others have a rough sketch and an idea they want to make real. This concept gives both a comfortable starting point, without expecting everyone to speak the language of fabrication.

● ● ●AFTERHOURS / STUDIO CONCEPT↗

03 / MUSIC & CREATIVE STUDIOS

AFTERHOURS

INDEPENDENT CONCEPT

A record-sleeve-inspired studio concept with an interactive vinyl record and two instrumental listening previews produced by TeddyBear.

Web design · Interactive motion

Explore the concept ↗
Behind the design +

The idea

The starting point was the feeling of making music after the rest of the world has gone quiet. I wanted a place where someone could settle in, put a track on, and get a sense of the person behind the sound.

How it took shape

Warm amber, dark surfaces, and a spinning record bring that late-night mood into the page. The tracks stay close at hand, so listening feels like the natural next step.

Fictional studio and independent design concept. The featured instrumentals were produced by TeddyBear; The Lead includes an R&B sample. Licence cards are illustrative and offer no music rights or prices. No client results are claimed.

● ● ●LITTLE JOY PLAY CO. / CONCEPT PREVIEW↗

04 / FAMILY CELEBRATIONS

Little Joy Play Co.

FEATURED PROJECT

A soft-play hire website with a gentle, playful feel, room for photographs and a simple way to plan a celebration.

Visual design · Responsive development · Interaction design

Read the case study ↗
Behind the design +

The idea

I imagined Little Joy as a welcoming place for parents to plan a celebration. I started with the things I’d want to know: what the play area looks like, what’s included and whether it will fit.

How it took shape

I borrowed the rounded shapes of soft-play equipment and paired them with gentle colours and generous photographs. Initially, I put everything on one long page, then gave each part its own space. I wanted parents to get a feel for Little Joy first and explore the details at their own pace.

I created Little Joy as an independent concept for a fictional business, with stock photography to help tell the story. The prices and packages are examples, and the enquiry form is a preview: nothing is sent or booked.

TEDEATS EVENTS / CONCEPT PREVIEW

05 / EVENT PLANNING & HOSTING

Tedeats Events

INDEPENDENT CONCEPT

An invitation-inspired events concept with real event photography, an unfolding letter and a bouquet tracing the planning journey.

Web design · Interactive motion

Explore the concept
Behind the design

The idea

A physical invitation makes an occasion feel real before anyone arrives. I used that first gesture to imagine Tedeats Events: a fictional planning and hosting brand where the website itself extends the invitation.

How it took shape

Cherry red, paper white and editorial type set the tone. Photographs emerge from an envelope and settle into a stack as you scroll. A bouquet follows a curved route through the planning stages, while layered stock photographs and drifting petals give the journey depth.

Tedeats Events is a fictional brand and independent portfolio concept. Credited stock photographs illustrate the mood, not Tedeats client work. The enquiry form is a local demonstration: nothing is sent, booked or paid for. No client commission, testimonials or business results are claimed.
