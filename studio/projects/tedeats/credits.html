Envelope artwork, typography & motion

The decorative red paper texture and gold wax seal were generated with an image tool using a visitor-provided envelope photograph as a visual reference. The flower that follows the planning path is the original generated bouquet cutout for this concept. The event photographs listed above remain real stock photography. Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are served locally; their licence notices are included with the site. The layered envelope, flower, photographic depth and code-drawn petals respond to native browser scrolling. Reduced motion shows a complete static gallery.

Your draft brief

The enquiry form creates a local preview and optional text download. It sends no messages, stores no details on a server and makes no bookings. Closing or refreshing the page discards the entered information.
