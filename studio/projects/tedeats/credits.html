A fictional occasion.

Tedeats Events is an independent website and brand concept by Tedits Studio. It is not an operating event business. The occasions, service descriptions and enquiry experience demonstrate a possible website; they do not represent client commissions, bookings or results.

Images

The dinner, garden celebration, launch reception, planning photograph and bouquet were generated with OpenAI’s image tool for this fictional concept in October 2026. They depict imagined scenes and people, and are not photographs of client work.

  • Dinner gathering — hero and invitation photograph.
  • Garden celebration — invitation photograph and occasion preview.
  • Launch reception — invitation photograph and occasion preview.
  • Floral preparations — planning journey and about section.
  • Cherry and ivory bouquet — scroll-following journey marker.

Typography & motion

Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are served locally. Their licence notices are included with the site. The envelope and bouquet motion use native browser scrolling, with a complete static alternative for reduced motion.

Your draft brief

The enquiry form creates a local preview and optional text download. It sends no messages, stores no details on a server and makes no bookings. Closing or refreshing the page discards the entered information.
