# Rivet & Beam — steel supply & fabrication website concept

**Status:** Independent concept for a fictional business. No real-company commission, approval, or endorsement.

**Role:** Website design and front-end implementation by Tedits Studio.

**Brief:** Explore how a materials business could help visitors browse a catalogue and describe a standard or custom fabrication requirement clearly.

**Design:** A navy, warm-white, and amber system, strong typography, material photography, and a restrained catalogue. Persistent concept labelling and photography credits keep its status explicit.

**Enquiry experience:** Catalogue actions preselect a material. Custom or uncertain requests reveal a project-description field. Dimensions and quantity are optional, with Not sure yet choices. Contractors can select sketches/drawings locally and review an editable summary. This demonstrates the interaction only: no price, quote, order, upload, email, or message is generated.

**Assets:** Original fictional wordmark and verified Pexels photography; provenance in concept-assets/SOURCES.md. Images illustrate categories and are not evidence of company stock, premises, employees, or work.

**Validation:** Responsive layouts and local interaction paths checked in Edge. No business outcomes, conversion improvements, client testimonials, or commercial results are claimed.

**Portfolio description:** An industrial website concept pairing a clear material catalogue with a flexible enquiry flow for standard supplies and custom fabrication. Fictional business; independently designed and developed.
