Little Joy
Play Co.

Premium soft-play hire, designed around parents.

A warm, playful website concept that makes the practical parts of planning a little celebration feel simple.

Explore the website ↗

A self-initiated concept for a fictional business.

The project
Independent concept
The work
Web design & development
The audience
Parents & event organisers
The website
Five focused pages

Small guests.
Clear choices.

Parents need to see what they’re hiring, compare prices and understand whether a setup will fit their venue. The design gives those decisions room, with large photographs and clear package details.

Soft-play arches became the frames for the imagery. Cream, butter, peach and sage bring a gentle sense of play, paired with expressive Fraunces headings and readable DM Sans text.

Room to breathe.

The first version put everything on one long homepage. The revised structure keeps a short introduction on Home, with full Packages, Gallery, FAQs and Booking pages available from the navigation.

The homepage went from roughly nine desktop screens to four. That is a layout comparison, rather than a claim about visitor behaviour or conversion.

Useful little details.

Choosing a package carries its name, price and space requirements into the enquiry page. Required fields, email and event dates are checked before a local preview is shown.

Native accordions, visible keyboard focus, gallery focus return and reduced-motion support keep the experience usable. The static site can run on GitHub Pages without a paid backend.
