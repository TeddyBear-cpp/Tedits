# Answers without the back-and-forth

Approved standalone first sample. No portfolio edits or deployment.

- Framework: Remotion + React/SVG, explicitly chosen by the user.
- Output: 4-second silent MP4, 1080 × 1080, 60fps (240 frames), with poster and editable source.
- Reference: supplied Parto Motion film. Borrow curved trajectories, kinetic timing and soft settling. Draw original artwork.
- Palette: charcoal #4A4A4A, ivory #FFFFE3, grey #CBCBCB, slate #6D8196.
- Typography: locally hosted DM Sans and Syne from the existing brand files.
- Story: scattered Services / Prices / FAQs elements gather into one clear website, briefly resolve, and return via a continuous visual thread.
- Motion: staggered spring settling and a drawn browser frame. Every visual is a deterministic function of the frame. User revision: remove the orbiting ball, its trail and orbit guide from both films.
- Loop: periodic motion at the boundary; the pose at frame 240 equals frame 0. Do not duplicate frame 0 at frame 239.
- Sound: none, intended as an automatic website-card loop.
- Delivery review: render stills, inspect movement, verify metadata and seam, then deliver the video for user review before any page work.

## Beats

0.00–0.55s: three separate information fragments introduce the problem.
0.55–1.75s: curved paths gather the fragments into a simple website frame, with staggered arrival.
1.75–2.75s: the organised information settles; a small cursor point connects the rows.
2.75–3.80s: the website unwinds and the fragments return along their paths.
3.80–4.00s: the floating fragments continue smoothly through the seam into the next cycle.
