# Build trust

Third standalone Remotion + SVG film. Six seconds, 1080 × 1080, 60fps, silent H.264. Same brand colours and fonts. No orbiting ball, trail or orbit guide. No website edits or deployment.

## Story

A simple website assembles. A project example lifts out to the left, then a client-review card settles below the right side. Five stars reveal in sequence, followed by “Earn their trust.” word by word. The proof cards return and the website releases to its initial outline.

The artwork and review card are generic illustrations, not a real customer testimonial or an assertion of an actual rating.

## Timing

- 0.00–0.33: a faint website outline anchors the opening.

- 1.73–2.80: the project example travels out along a curved path and settles.
- 2.17–3.17: the review card arrives, with a staggered five-star reveal.
- 2.75–3.37: the closing caption arrives word by word.
- 3.37–4.63: the composition holds for reading.
- 4.63–5.80: cards return and the site clears back to its starting outline.
- 5.80–6.00: the starting pose holds through the loop boundary.
